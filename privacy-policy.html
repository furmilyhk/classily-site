Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Classily is a study app for students. There is no account and no login. We operate no user database, and your notes belong to you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no sign-up. We cannot see your notes, and we do not know who you are.

Where your work lives

  • Your timetable, lesson notes, photos, PDFs, homework and recordings are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud container, which syncs between your devices. That is your Apple account and your storage; we have no access to it.

Recordings and transcripts

  • Lesson recordings stay on your device. They are never uploaded and are not synced to iCloud.
  • Turning a recording into text uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio does not leave your phone.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days. Your notes, transcripts and photos are kept.

The AI features — what does leave your device

This is the one part of Classily that sends anything off your device, and only when you choose to use it.

  • When you ask the AI a question, your question is sent to our processing endpoint and on to Google's Gemini API, which generates the answer.
  • If you turn on "use my notes to answer", the relevant parts of your own lesson notes are sent with the question so the answer can refer to them.
  • When you have a photo read for text (OCR), that image is sent for processing.
  • Everything else — searching, markup, timetable, homework — happens on your device and sends nothing.

We do not store your questions, your notes or your photos on our side. They are passed through to generate that one answer and are not retained by us, are not linked to any identity, and are never sold or used for advertising. Google processes the request under its API terms as a service provider.

If you would rather nothing left your device at all, simply do not use the AI features. Everything else in the app keeps working.

Camera, photos and microphone

The camera is used only when you photograph a board, a worksheet or a timetable. The photo library is read only for images you pick. The microphone is used only while you are recording a lesson. None of these are accessed in the background.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a valid subscription exists.

Children

Classily is intended for secondary school students and above. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your control

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Notes kept in iCloud can be removed from your Apple account in Settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date.
